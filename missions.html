Mission 1: Trial of Possession

DATE: 7.12.3134

TO: Hermes Company Lance Leaders

FROM: Defenders of Zollikofen Command & Control

SUBJ: This One's For All the Marbles

Howdy, Mr. Cade, Mr. Jeff, Mr. Battletechson,

It's been a helluva week, huh. If you had told me three months ago where we'd be today, I would've laughed at you. But that's life, ain't it?

To recap, in case y'all've been tryin' your best to live under a rock: Clan Jade Falcon's Omicron Galaxy is invading Zollikofen. Our resident Knight of the Republic of the Sphere, Ronan Beneventi, has somehow managed to corral the VIII Principes Guards, the Swordsworn, the Stormhammers, Dragon's Fury, and Bannson's Raiders into a loose alliance we're calling the Defenders of Zollikofen (we didn't have time to come up with a cool name). Everyone's trying their best to work together, but not everyone agrees on the right path.

Thankfully, they got us. Due to your performance during the race to the cache, Defenders Command & Control (which now includes yours truly) has decided to give y'all a promotion. Congrats, you're all lance leaders now! HR's updated your paystubs to say sergeant on it. 'Course, now I'm captain, so you still get to listen to me.

Y'all have been promoted because you'll be leading Hermes Company. Cpt. Rochette describes it as a “self-organizing, flexible, tactical response and support company.” I like to call it you three and whoever you decide to bring along for each mission. Kommandant Der Voorde isn't too happy that all of Defiance Industries' ammo is given to you fellas, but we're all makin' tough calls here.

Speakin' of tough calls, your first mission is watching one. Knight Beneventi declared a Trial of Possession with Galaxy Commander Urakov Hazen. If our guy wins, Zollikofen will remain free. If not . . . Well, we'll have to figure that one out. . .

Anyway, y'all will be part of the “Circle of Equals.” Choose your 'Mechs - ideally a mixed force - and let's hit the road. Maybe pop some popcorn first. I got a feelin' Beneventi will see it all through.

Let's not start a war,

“Doc” Sam Willis

Captain, Hermes Company

PhD in Supply Chain Management
