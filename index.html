Welcome!

This is the home page for a BattleTech campaign, Turning Point Zollikofen: Talons of Urakov!

Players of the campaign can use this website to read information about/for the campaign, and build a list with the allowed units

The year is 3134. Clan Jade Falcon's Omicron Galaxy, led by Urakov Hazen and his lackey, Casia Pryde, have invaded the planet of Zollikofen. If they are successful, Terra itself may very well be at risk of Falcon invasion. Their only resistance are the jackals tearing the Republic of the Sphere apart: The courageous Swordsworn, the determined Stormhammers, the tenacious Dragon's Fury, the cunning Bannson's Raiders, and first among equals, the Republic's VIII Principes Guards. Among the defenders are the mercenaries Lethal Company, whose Mercury Lance (the players) have been promoted to sergeants. They now lead Hermes Company, a quick-response task force of the best and brightest troubleshooters the Defenders of Zollikofen have. This campaign follows the exploits of Hermes Company as they leap from the frying pan and into a series of fires. Can Clan Jade Falcon be repelled? Is the Republic of the Sphere doomed? Who knows!

Rules-wise, this is an Alpha Strike campaign. Each week players have 150 PV to build a lance as they see fit. Lance formations (see AS rulebook, pg 116 onwards) are allowed. If a player has leftover PV from their lance, they may donate it to another player (i.e. player A has a 125 PV list, so gives their remaining PV to player B so they have 175 to play around with). There is one notable house rule though: If a 'Mech is destroyed in a mission, it is gone and can no longer be taken! They will be moved from the Hangar page to the Graveyard page. If battle armor or vehicles are destroyed, they will not be perma-killed, because they are more replacable/repairable than a BattleMech.
