Build a Force

Listbuilding restrictions:

Before each mission, each player builds a lance (or "list"). These three lances are collectively Hermes Company. As of 11/23/2026, you can add units by going to the Hangar page.

You may select 0-4 Vehicles or BattleMechs whose total PV is less than or equal 150. You may select one unit per unit card (for example, you cannot take the Valkyrie C twice, but you can take two Hetzers because there are two Hetzer unit cards).

An exception to that are the three Battle Armors: the Elementals, Inner Sphere Battle Armor, and Thunderbirds. Each player may take 0-3, and they do not count towards to the unit maximum (i.e. 4 'Mechs and 3 Elementals is kosher, so is 1 'Mech and 3 Thunderbirds [don't do this]). However, the PV limit must still be observed.

Colored borders mean that unit is mutually exclusive with units of the same border. For example, Cougar A and Cougar B cannot be in the same company. For the teal-borders (i.e. Locust, King Crab. etc.), the players combined can take up to 3, since those represent the 'Mechs the player characters can pilot (and there's 3 of them)

If you want, comb through the Alpha Strike: Commander's Edition rulebook (pages 116 to 121) and build your force to adhere to one of the Formations. Their rules are allowed for this campaign.

Each player must submit their lance to the game master to make sure everything's valid. Ideally, the company is less than or equal to 450 PV. If one player has leftover PV, they can "donate" it to another player. For example, if Player A's lance is 125 PV, they can give Player B the remainder so they have 175 PV.
