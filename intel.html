Unit Digests

Omicron Galaxy

Greetings, Lance Leaders,

According to old DMI records, there was never a Clan Jade Falcon Omicron Galaxy. I guess that needs updating now. My staff and I believe Omicron Galaxy was stood up as a side effect of the Falcon desant.

From the Stormhammers' BattleROM footage, we've identified the livery of Gyrfalcon, Alpha, and Zeta Galaxies. Together, the three ravaged Prefecture IX and have made incursions into Prefecture VIII. Eventually, Clan Jade Falcon carved out a foothold in Republic space — the desant. However, many of their warriors are allegedly green, dissident, or dishonorable. These Jade Falcons also need to hold an area with limited supplies and reinforcements.

Therefore, we're fairly confident Omicron Galaxy was cobbled together to increase tactical and strategic flexibility. A fourth Galaxy allows the other Galaxy Commanders to maintain their grip on the desant while still sending out troops to raid for supplies or sow fear. And, of course, a new Galaxy provides an opportunity to weed out the weak and blood some veterans.

A crucial piece of evidence is Omicron Galaxy's smaller size compared to its peers. If Omicron is one big melting pot for broken commands, bondsmen, old solahma troops, and fresh sibkos, then that would explain why it didn't exist until after the Battle of Skye.

Regardless of their origin, Omicron Galaxy is here on Zollikofen. They were likely drawn here because of its old BattleMech cache and its strategic location close to Terra. Clan culture holds a pseudospiritual belief that the first clan to take Terra shall become ilClan, the Clan of Clans, and lead humanity to a golden age under the aegis of a new Star League. Of course, we live on Terra, and if these ruthless Falcons get there, it'll be quite literally Hell on Earth.

Each Galaxy is made of several Clusters, which are in turn made up of several Stars, which are made up of 5 BattleMechs (or a Point of Elementals, or two vehicles). These are led by a Galaxy Commander, Star Colonels, and Star Commanders (respectively).

We haven't gotten much intel yet on the lower ranks of leadership, but Galaxy Commander Urakov Hazen is well-known to us (relatively speaking, at least). His Hazen Bloodname suggests he probably follows the Mongol Doctrine, the same line of thinking that led to nukes being launched on Skye, courtesy of the insane Galaxy Commander Malvina Hazen. Expect brutal fighting, with no quarter taken or given. Urakov is likely the most dangerous person on Zollikofen - strong, ambitious, and driven. This world will be his Rubicon.

All Clanners, however, have a sense of honor. For example, none of them will engage in melee until their opponent does it first. Additionally, their zellbrigen will dictate what assets they bid away for a fair fight. However, this new generation of Jade Falcons typically disregards zellbrigen toward Spheroids like us, though it might still be a useful asset. Knight Beneventi wields enough respect to challenge Urakov to a one-on-one Trial of Possession for all of Zollikofen. Our hopes ride with the Knight - but it never hurts to have a backup plan.

The Winged Keshik

Greetings, Lance Leaders,

As you may already know, the Keshik is the traditional honor guard of a Khan or Galaxy Commander. They are typically the best of the best, and sworn to stay near their charge and defend them with their lives. Each Keshik ranges in size, but is usually around a Cluster in strength and commanded by a Star Colonel. The Winged Keshik's leader is Star Colonel Casia Pryde, and is also Urakov Hazen's right-hand.

Urakov, however, has taken a different approach to his guard. The Winged Keshik is still present wherever their Commander goes, but their warriors are also seeded throughout Omicron. Scouts and survivors report 'Mechs belonging to the Winged Keshik can be seen working with frontline Stars, and sometimes even commanding them. Why they do this is currently unknown, but my team has a few theories, ranked from most probable to least:

  1. Urakov wants to keep his Keshik honed and inspire other warriors to their greatness.
  2. Urakov wants eyes and ears throughout Omicron to guard against dissension.
  3. The Winged Keshik's presence is a fear tactic.
  4. Members of the Winged Keshik are being deployed for leadership experience that will come into play when Urakov is gone.
  5. Omicron Galaxy is small enough to warrant regular deployment of the Winged Keshik.

Whatever the case, watch for the Winged Keshik's BattleMechs. You can identify them by their purple-and-khaki livery and their tendency to use 'Mechs named after birds. Failing that, they're often seen commanding Stars or executing bold plans. We advise you to always keep your head on a swivel when they're around. Good luck out there, MechWarriors.
