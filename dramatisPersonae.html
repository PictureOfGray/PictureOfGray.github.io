Turning Point: Zollikofen: Talons of Urakov

Dramatis Personae

Clan Jade Falcon

Galaxy Commander Urakov Hazen

Not much is known about Galaxy Commander Urakov Hazen. However, Urakov has proven himself a ruthless commander befitting of the Bloodname and the modern Jade Falcons. If he conquers Zollikofen, which many believe to be a "when", he'll use this stronghold to raid further into the Republic, perhaps even striking at Terra itself. For now, at any rate, Clan Jade Falcon is here to remove all opposition and salvage as much as they can. He pilots a 'Mech called the Jade Hawk, “Hannibal.”

Defenders of Zollikofen

Do note all these characters should be Skill 3

Knight Ronan Beneventi, VIII Principes Guards

Knight Beneventi has the unenviable position of taking 6 different groups and forging them into a unified fighting force. Beneventi's family served in the Clan Wars of the 3050s, and he is rightfully worried about Clan Jade Falcon bearing down. He prefers to lead from the front, but has set up an intelligence shop to make sure his C2 is still intact should he go down. He'll go down swinging though, along with the entire planet if that's what it takes. He pilots a Dire Wolf, “Ozymandias.”

Captain Beth Rochette, VIII Principes Guards

Prefecture VIII didn't see the fighting of the Capellan Crusades, so their Principes Guards didn't gain experience fighting a near-peer opponent. Thus, Cpt. Rochette is drilling her troops the best she can. Rochette's been accused of routinely placing Principes Guards soldiers in command of operations, but in her defence, they are the most experienced. While Beneventi is in meetings, she makes sure everyone else falls in line. Those who don't stick together, hang together and all that. She pilots an Executioner, "Atom Bomb Baby.”

Chu-sa (Lt. Col) Haruta el-Mona, Dragon's Fury

If Chu-sa el-Mona had her way, everyone would have struck at Omicron Galaxy the second they had the chance. Now, they're stuck in a drawn-out campaign, and she knows they won't last long as long as the others are calling the shots. Of all the jackals, she protests against the Defenders' plans the most, but it does often lead to a stronger strategy. However, Dragon's Fury units are typically among the most zealous and damaged. She pilots a Marauder II, "Usseewa.”

Tai-i (Captain) Hayashi Mannik, Dragon's Fury

Since Omicron landed, Tai-i Mannik's become the closest the Defenders have to a chaplain. Mannik's rhetoric has turned awfully pro-Republic, or at least pro-unity, for the time being. Whenever he leads a force, Mannik commands like a preacher the pulpit, lacing his orders with colorful phrases and insults. For his part, his "patriotic sermons" have boosted morale a fair bit. He pilots a Warhammer IIC, "Black Knife.”

Lieutenant Colonel “Hellish” Helena Sissako, Bannson's Raiders

If you would ask Lt. Col Sissako her thoughts on this whole invasion, she would say that the whole job's gone to Hell. The only reason Bannson's Raiders haven't retreated is because there aren't any free JumpShips above Zollikofen. However, Sissako knows her Raiders can't survive on their own, so they've joined the Defenders until the time comes to run. When it comes to special operations, Bannson's Raiders has the most influence in the war room. She pilots a King Crab, "Dorothy.”

Captain Tristan Nallbani, Bannson's Raiders

Cpt. Nallbani has taken up the role of managing the nascent Quartermaster's Department. After all, if you want to manage your assets, ask an accountant. Of course sometimes a few items get misplaced or overallocated to Raiders-related forces. But even so, his adaptability and sheer will has ensured the Defenders don't starve on the way to Fullbright. He pilots an Akuma, "Leave Without Pay.”

Leutnant-Colonel Ambessa Isaia, Stormhammers

The Stormhammers are treating this invasion as an existential threat, chief among them Leutnant-Colonel Isaia. She served during the Battle of Skye, and has seen the horrors the Jade Falcons can inflict firsthand. However, she's also familiar with the power supply lines have over an army, and has been advocating for a slow, grinding campaign -- much to the chagrin of Chu-sa el-Mona. She pilots an Atlas, "Supplied Demand.”

Kommandant Zivota Der Voorde, Stormhammers

Kommandant Der Voorde has been authorized with sharing supplies from Defiance Industries, and has also shared the intelligence the Stormhammers have gathered on Clan Jade Falcon. Without Der Voorde, the intel surrounding Omicron would look far more disorganized. He also serves faithfully as an aide to Lt. Col. Isaia, which has caused some to call him "The Intern." He pilots an Emperor, "Koenigsjaeger.”

Major Bryson Dupont, Swordsworn

Major Dupont has taken to thinking he is the only one thinking about the long term. Sure, the Jade Falcons need to be defeated, but what happens after that? To him, this is a grand opportunity to position Swordsworn as the saviors of the Republic. According to some reports, Swordsworn and Dragon's Fury pilots are trying to outcompete each other for kills and glory. Dupont denies all allegations. He pilots a Devastator, "Babel.”

Captain Mel Waterleaf, Swordsworn

A sarcastic "Thy will be done" could best sum up Captain Waterleaf. He ensures orders are carried out to the letter, and then adds a few more sentences to make sure the Swordsworn end up in a good light. Unfortunately, that's bungled a few missions, and Defenders HQ has him over hot water. He pilots a Gunslinger, "David's Slings.”

Doc Willis, Lethal Company

Like the rest of Mercury Lance, Doc Willis has received a battlefield promotion. Now, he manages the recruitment and deployment of Hermes Company, a tactical response force comprised of members from all the jackals. For once, the good Doc is unsure if he'll get out of this alive, and has taken to collecting various lucky charms. At least he has a new ride. He pilots a Merlin, "Secretariat.”
